Written for: the editor of the story (the user). Nothing here is public. Decided by the main session at the user's direction ("the spine isn't exhaustive, the story is much broader than what the documents can support, you decide").

## Decision

- **Reader:** a non-Russian general reader (press, policy staff). The sanctions-gap table is the specialist deliverable, not the story.
- **Spine:** *a donor foundation, a state-linked centre and a marketplace that together turn equipment for the war into "help for the front".* Told through one organisation, Фонд «Наша Правда», because it is the best documented in pictures, money and its own words.
- **Rule for the whole story:** each chapter says only what its documents support. Where the documents stop, the chapter says so on the page (the limits are part of the story, not a footnote). The story is broader than the record; the page is not.
- **Words:** «благотворительная помощь» is the foundation's own term and is quoted, never adopted as ours. No chapter implies combat use from a delivery.
