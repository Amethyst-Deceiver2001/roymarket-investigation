# Kherson "human safari": the units named, and where our case touches them (24.10.2026)

Requested by the user. **Everything here is a lead graded by who says it; nothing is a registry fact.** Documents read in full in their own language: the SBU's notice (Ukrainian), the CIR/DW write-up, the HRW report (passages on units and manufacturers), and the Архангел posts below (Russian, from `data/roymarket.db`). Not read: the DW documentary, the UN Commission's own papers (OHCHR blocks this machine; UN News and secondary summaries only), the Mezha report (403).

## 1. The campaign, as documented

- **What is documented.** Drones carrying small explosives are used against civilians and civilian vehicles in Kherson and along the Dnipro's right bank. The CIR/DW investigation logged **over 100 attacks on vehicles and homes in Beryslav between September 2023 and July 2024, with nearly 130 civilians reportedly injured and 16 dead** (CIR write-up, read). HRW (June 2025) documents the same pattern in Kherson city. The UN Commission of Inquiry is reported (secondary only) to have found crimes against humanity of murder in May 2025; **we have not read its paper.**
- **The label.** "Human safari" is the Ukrainian and Western press name. In Russian military slang «сафари» means **hunting enemy drones**: the Архангел channel advertises «сафари на вражеские борты на безопасном удалении от ЛБС» (проект Архангел 10737, 02.09.2026) and Sudoplatov's channel «тепловизионные прицелы для ночного сафари» (6994, 24.11.2025). **No post in our scrape uses "safari" for the Kherson campaign** (11 hits for «сафари», none about it). The word cannot be used to find our network's side of it.

## 3. Where our case touches them

### 3.1 The ЦБСТ-tied Архангел network recruits for the 205th brigade (the strongest link)

**Correction (24.10.2026):** the ЦБСТ posts 1976 (accelerator finalist) and 2095 (EU 20th package) name «Архангел» the interceptor-drone maker, which the registry resolves to ООО «Архангел» (ИНН 9714079108, director Шатракова) by its director's name; **no document on file links that company to the training project that runs `projectArchangel`.** A brand never merges entities. The project's own tie to ЦБСТ is `t-open`: ЦБСТ's post 1562 (25.11.2025) says a resident's anti-drone simulator mode runs at the project's Туапсе training centre. The project's place in Барс-Сармат rests on Рогозин's post of 22.04.2025 [s6915]. Earlier text in this note that called the project «ЦБСТ-tied» through 1976 and 2095 is wrong on that point. The channel `projectArchangel` states, in its own words:

- **01.12.2025 (9067):** «Заключай контракт с Министерством обороны и неси дальнейшую службу в подразделении БПЛА 205-ой Гв ОМСБр.» The course ends: «Выпускной экзамен — это Твое первое боевое задание, на котором Ты … поразишь свою первую цель.» Signed «205-я Бригада».
- **08.03.2026 (9685):** greetings «от лица службы БпС 205 омсбр».
- **08–12.03.2026 (9689 to 9727, five identical posts):** «Требуются специалисты на демонтажные работы … Вахта по спецконтракту в 205 бригаду на 1 год … Подъемные 3.4 миллиона рублей … Объекты на Херсонском направлении.» A military contract advertised as «демонтажные работы» (euphemism, STYLE_GUIDE list).

**What this establishes:** a ЦБСТ-tied training project steers its graduates into a brigade that CIR/DW (2024) listed among three units whose drones were filmed in the Beryslav attacks, and describes that brigade's drone work against the Kherson oblast in the project's own words (strike drones included). **What it does not establish:** that any Архангел graduate took part in an attack on civilians; that the brigade did; that CIR's "205th Motorized Rifle Battalion" is this brigade; or what the brigade did after 2024. The posts are the project's own statements (grade rep).

Related, also from the channel's own account:

### 3.2 Sudoplatov: a manufacturer HRW names, and a rival of Архангел

HRW identified the Russian maker **Sudoplatov**'s VT-40 drone in one documented attack on a civilian in Kherson, and noted the Sudoplatov channel's reposts of VT-40 attack videos «claiming to show» use in Kherson on 03.08.2024; the maker had not answered HRW's letter. Our registry already holds the lead that Архангел offered a build variant with «судоплатовских комплектующих (ВТ 40)» alongside its standard kit, from its private chat (an open question on `proekt-kb-arkhangel`, 04.09.2026). Sudoplatov has no record of its own; 101 posts in our scrape carry the name, most of them in its own channel. The two make different things (Архангел sells anti-drone interceptors), so a shared component is not shared use.

### 3.3 «Наша Правда» deliveries to the Kherson direction

From the fund's own posts, as recorded in `deliveries.json` (these posts predate this machine's scrape, so the wording is the registry's recorded text, not re-read here; self-reported, single source):
- **07.05.2024 (post 280):** «доставили **100 ударных дронов** бойцам **49-й армии**, выполняющим задачи на Херсонском направлении.»
- **10–11.05.2024 (posts 282, 283):** radio-electronic-warfare sets, Mavic copters and «ударные дроны» to the 345th regiment of the 104th division, the 76th division, the 100th brigade of the 42nd division and the 58th Army, «на Запорожском и Херсонском направлениях».
- **13.01.2025 (ЦБСТ post 369):** «Капюшон» jammers to servicemen of the «Центр» and «Днепр» groups. **15.01.2025 (post 382):** «Булат» detectors and «Капюшон» jammers for Kherson-oblast municipal heads and electricity repair brigades (civilian recipients).

**Why the 49th Army matters:** English Wikipedia (a lead, not a source for a parent) says the 205th brigade belongs to the 49th Army. If a source that states the pairing is found, then the fund delivered strike drones to the formation that includes the brigade named by CIR/DW, on the same front and **inside CIR/DW's window (Sept 2023–July 2024)**. That would be a formation-level overlap, not proof that those drones reached the 205th, or were used on civilians. The deliveries say «бойцам 49-й армии» and nothing more.

## 4. What we can and cannot say

Can say, attributed:
- CIR and DW (2024) found that three Russian units, including «the 205th Motorized Rifle Battalion», appeared to operate drones in the Beryslav attacks, and said they could not prove who ordered or carried them out.
- The SBU (June 2026) notified ten men of the 404th regiment of suspicion of war crimes in Kherson.
- A training project that ЦБСТ's own channel names as a resident recruits for the 205th brigade, calls it «гроза Херсонской области», and says its people serve there.
- «Наша Правда» reports delivering 100 strike drones to the 49th Army on the Kherson direction in May 2024.

Cannot say: that Архангел, ЦБСТ, «Наша Правда» or their donors took part in or enabled attacks on civilians; that any named unit did what the SBU alleges; that the 205th brigade and CIR's "205th battalion" are the same; that a delivered drone was used against civilians.
