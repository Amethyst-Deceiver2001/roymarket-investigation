## 4. Model (schema v8, pilot — built 2026-10-01)

See `scripts/graph_contract.py` and `data/registry/programs.json`.

- `programs.json` holds records of kind `program`, `event` and `group`. Entities stay the source of truth for who belongs: an entity's `memberships[]` (residency, accelerator) and `participations[]` (events) carry the dated, graded claims.
- Edges: `operates`, `organises`, `resident_of`, `participated_in`, `part_of`, `member_of`. Grade in `confidence` as for `sells_for` (doc / press / inferred).
- A group has no legal identity; the family stays three separate entities. Programs and events are nodes because they have many members and their own attributes (claims, terms, editions).
- Claimed counts stay claims on the program record with source and date ("30+ residents, as of 2026-09-30, self-reported"); named residents attach one by one as they surface. No stub entities for unnamed residents.
- Deliberately not done: a КЦПН entity; named residents from the site (none published); a `hub` for the БАС project (it is a program node).
