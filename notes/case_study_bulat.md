# «Булат»: a Petersburg detector maker with hidden owners, a sole trader in Khimki, and ₽28.2m of МЧС contracts awarded without a tender

2026-09-20. Every claim carries a `[sNNNN]` source id, resolved in the table at the end. Russian is quoted verbatim from archived documents and posts; English is analysis. **Registry position: tier manufacturer for all three companies. The ЦБСТ tie is on the maker ООО «Тримикс» only, from ЦБСТ's own channel [s7596] {doc}. The dealer and the sole trader have no ЦБСТ tie of their own; the path to ЦБСТ runs through the maker.**

## The finding

«Булат» is a portable drone detector made by ООО «Тримикс» (ИНН 7802942873), registered in Санкт-Петербург on 21.07.2023. Its ЕГРЮЛ entry hides its director and its owners: «Доступ к сведениям ограничен» [s6749]. It is resold in Московская область by ООО «ПОКРОВ» (ИНН 5038189259, Пушкино), which 3mx.ru lists as a dealer [s0558]. Вадим Цыганов owns 100% of ООО «ПОКРОВ» and is its director [s6799]. He is also a registered sole trader, ИП Цыганов Вадим Борисович (ИНН 504405055721), in Химки [s6800]. Two МЧС buyers awarded the sole trader two contracts worth ₽28.176m without a tender, for «Булат» detectors and «Покров» jammers [s1416, s1417]. The company was listed in Ukraine's sanctions register by presidential decree on 09.05.2025 [s7873]; both МЧС contracts with the sole trader are later than the company's registration and one of them is later than its listing. ЦБСТ's own channel lists «детекторы «Булат»» among its residents' exhibits [s7596].

All four were bought from a sole supplier. The two МЧС contracts and ЕП 2-25 rest on the same ground: пункт 9 части 1 статьи 93 of 44-ФЗ, purchase «вследствие аварии, обстоятельств непреодолимой силы, для предупреждения … и (или) ликвидации чрезвычайной ситуации» [s1416, s1417, s3672]. ЕП 2-25 states that it was not concluded for a state defence order. The ₽28.176m headline is the sum of the two sole-trader contracts; the company's two contracts add ₽2.8m.

- **Unit prices.** ЕП 2-25 prices «Булат v4» at ₽99,000 a kit and the version with external antennas at ₽105,100 [s3672].
- **Late delivery.** The ЕП 3-25 card gives a term of 17.11.2025 [s1417]; the acceptance act records delivery on 24.12.2025 [s1464], read by OCR. The contract card was updated on 10.03.2026; the reason for the gap is not shown.

## Sanctions

- **ООО «ПОКРОВ»:** listed in Ukraine's State Register of Sanctions under presidential decree 301/2025 of 09.05.2025, annex 2, position 50, in force for ten years, with a ban on public and defence procurement {doc} [s7873]. The signed extract is on file.
- **ООО «Тримикс»:** an OpenSanctions match by ИНН cites several regimes as sources, among them Ukraine's and France's {open}. No primary text is on file.
- **Цыганов personally:** the registry holds a match from a mirror of Ukraine's register {open}. No primary text is on file.

## What is not established

- Who owns ООО «Тримикс» today. The register is restricted and the dossier is partial and undated in its provenance.
- Whether the sole trader's contracts were routed to him instead of the company because of the company's listing. The dates are on file; no document states a reason.
- What the ₽110.4m of ООО «ПОКРОВ» revenue for 2025 consists of. The company's contracts on file for 2025 total ₽2.3m.
- Whether the sanctions listed by OpenSanctions against «Тримикс» and Цыганов exist in the primary registers.
- Trimix's revenue for 2024 and 2025. No filing is on file.
- Whether the sole-supplier ground, meant for emergencies, fits a jammer bought in September 2025.
