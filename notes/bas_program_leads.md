## 2. What the program pays for (portal wording, to be confirmed in the decisions)

- 48 НПЦ БАС planned by 2030; 18 accredited by Росавиация; residents are SMEs chosen by Фонд НТИ. Фонд НТИ «определил 148 победителей» in 2024–2025.
- Grants: up to ₽30m prototypes; up to ₽60m pilot samples; up to ₽300m serial production. Up to ₽100m design documentation; up to ₽250m certification; up to ₽1bn per НПЦ.
- Subsidy up to ₽2m per drone sold at a discount, paid through Минпромторг. A 2025 additional round was about ₽177m (ComNews search result, lead only).
- Demand side: ГТЛК leasing 3.5–8.5%, loans up to ₽1bn at 3–5%; up to ₽95k compensation per flight hour (Минпромторг opened a selection for it in April 2025, lead only).
- Target: Russian drones 70% of the market by 2030, now 42%.
