# ФБК «Он вам не Димон» (2017): the 174 images, read against our records

Written 24.10.2026 by the session that pulled the images (`roymarket-investigation-a2`), at the main session's request. Companion to `notes/source_fbk_dimon_2017.md` (analysis of the text). **Nothing here is a registry fact.** Every exhibit is FBK's selection, captured from `dimon.navalny.com` in 2017 and reproduced at most 700 px wide; a screenshot of a SPARK screen or an extract is not the extract itself. Standing of each exhibit is "supports / partly / does not / illegible" for the FBK sentence it is offered for, and says nothing about whether the sentence is true.

## How this was read

- All 80 `role=content` images were looked at at the size we hold (700 px wide at most; 290 and 440 for the portraits). Three are mailbox screenshots and were not looked at (below). Four composites (43–46) contain mailbox excerpts; only the non-mailbox half was looked at.
- The 94 `role=gallery_full` images were looked at on contact sheets, and **19 of them turned out to be documents, drawings, maps or screenshots, not photos** (the manifest's role is a page-layout flag, not a content test). The ten that matter (documents) were opened at full size; all sit in section C. The rest get one line (section D).
- Text is copied as printed, Russian as printed, including typos. Where the print is small, a reading is marked "(small print)". Nothing is filled in for an unreadable field.
- No name is taken from a face. Where an image carries a caption naming a person, that is FBK's caption and is said so.
- Personal data of people named only in this piece (private ИНН, dates of birth, home addresses, phone numbers, personal e-mail addresses) is not copied, except where the main session's brief asked for it and it sits in a registry capture (noted at the item).

### A1. The Росреестр extract for the 2014 transfer ДАР → Градислава (Миловка)

**It is not a content image.** FBK's text names it only as a link («выписка из реестра недвижимости»); the page's one picture of it is the uncaptioned **gallery n=4** (Chapter 1, «Как Медведев подарил дачу сам себе»). It is a **crop of one row**:

> вид зарегистрированного права; доля в праве: Собственность 37-37-08/244/2014-030 19.09.2014
> дата государственной регистрации права: 19.09.2014

> дата, номер и основание государственной регистрации перехода (прекращения) права: Договор пожертвования недвижимого имущества

- **FBK sentence:** «В 2014 году «Дар» передал «Миловку» в собственность другому фонду — … «Градислава»» and «В ней сказано, что «Дар» просто подарил старинную усадьбу «Градиславе»» (ch.1).
- **Standing: partly.** It shows Градислава (ИНН and ОГРН as in our record) entered as owner on 19.09.2014 on a «Договор пожертвования недвижимого имущества». The crop **does not show**: the object (no cadastral number, address, area or name; "Миловка" and "Плёс" do not appear), the previous owner (row 2.1 is cut off, so ДАР is not shown as the transferor; only «в государственной собственности» is visible at the top of the crop, which is the end of a row above), a value, or any price. The state-registration number's prefix `37-…` is consistent with Ивановская область (Плёс), but that is our inference, not something the image says.
- **«Подарил» is FBK's gloss.** The printed basis is «пожертвование» (a donation to a charity), a different legal word from «дарение».
- Against our record: Градислава's ИНН 7704278421, ОГРН 1117799007050 and the full name match. We hold no property record for Миловка; this image alone does not make one.
- **To close the lead we need the whole extract** (the page links it), not this crop.
