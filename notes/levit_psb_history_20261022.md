## What this adds to oq022

1. **Левит was a shareholder of ПСБ after the merger**, at least at 30.06.2017
   (lead, percentage unknown). "Did not control" stays supported by the list
   (Promsvyaz Capital B.V., the Ананьевы vehicle, had 50.03%), but "no link" is
   too strong: the owners of Левит were minority shareholders of ПСБ until the
   2017-18 rescue, and creditors of its former owners afterwards.
2. **The 2015 deal was a share swap with deferred payment**, and the unpaid part
   became a claim against the Ананьевы. Левит's money therefore left ПСБ's
   former owners, not the state bank. Whether any Левит stake survived the
   rescue is not known from these sources.
3. **A separate thread.** ФБК's 2017 investigation said the owners of Новатэк put
   ₽33bn into ДАР's charter; Михельсон said Левит founded ДАР and he would keep
   supporting it; the head of ДАР in 2017 was Илья Елисеев (registry: person
   record with a regime-less sanctions entry). All three statements are
   press-level and from 2011-2017.
