The finding

Андрей Черезов owns 100% of two companies registered in the same building in Tula, on ул. Рязанская, д. 3Д: ООО «Русская лаборатория воздушного транспорта» (РЛВТ, ИНН 7100027464), registered 28.09.2022, and ООО «Воздушный трактат» (ИНН 7100046925), registered 28.11.2023 [s6727, s6728]. РЛВТ reports revenue of ₽110m for 2023, ₽562m for 2024 and ₽1.31bn for 2025, and a balance sheet of ₽4.22bn at the end of 2025 [s6532, s6533, s6534]. It makes the «Овод» FPV strike drones and the fibre-optic «Провод» . ЦБСТ's own channel called the drone «нашим флагманским резидентом» on 15.04.2025 . On 29.07.2026 Черезов was shot in a stairwell in Tula . The state contracts on file are four university purchases worth ₽72.65m, about 4% of the company's revenue for 2024 and 2025 [s3372, s3374, s3375, s3376].

Sanctions

  • EU, primary. Council Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1843 of 23 July 2026 lists, as entry 963 of the annex to Regulation 269/2014, «Russian Air Transport Laboratory» a.k.a. ООО «РЛВТ» (ИНН 7100027464, ОГРН 1227100012369). Its reasons: «a Russian developer and manufacturer of drones, including attack drones. Its products include first-person view (FPV) drones Ovod (Gadfly)», documented in combat «near Avdiivka on 4 July 2023» Primary doc . Council Regulation (EU) 2026/1848 of the same date adds «RLVT Tula», with the same ИНН and ОГРН and the address of the company, as entry 929 to Annex IV of Regulation 833/2014, dated 24.7.2026 Primary doc . The listing came six days before the shooting of 29.07.2026.
  • US, press only. The Insider reports that РЛВТ was sanctioned by the US in 2024, and by the EU in July 2026, «всего за несколько дней до покушения на Черезова» Press-reported . No US primary text is on file.
  • OpenSanctions matches the ИНН of РЛВТ and agrees with the EU entries Open .
  • GUR's page for Овод lists «FPV kamikaze» as the purpose .

The founder and the shooting

  • The official account. The Tula investigative committee said that on 29.07.2026 «в подъезде многоквартирного жилого дома на улице Михеева неустановленное лицо из огнестрельного оружия произвело выстрелы в тульского бизнесмена. Потерпевший госпитализирован» Press-reported .
  • Who was hit. Mash names the victim: «Пострадавший — Андрей Черезов» Press-reported . Овод's own statement says he was shot three times Open.
  • ЦБСТ's account. ЦБСТ says the enemy organised the attack Open. The investigative committee's own statement speaks only of an «неустановленное лицо» .
  • Mash's theory. It suggests the gunman may have mistaken the victim for the second co-owner of a security firm, «также Андрей из Тулы» Press-reported . That is Mash's speculation, not a finding.

What the company says about itself

  • Combat use. «Произвели. Испытали. Применили.» (08.07.2026) Open . On 18.08.2026 the channel says «Провод» flew «40 километров 400 метров» and destroyed a depot Open.
  • A unit of veterans. «Сегодня год с момента основания боевой группы "ОВОД"» (28.08.2026), and «Военные специалисты ОВОДА содействуют подразделениям МО РФ и других силовых служб в зоне СВО» Open .
  • Unit footage. On 10.06.2025 the channel reposts a report on the 88th brigade's flight battalion using «Овод» drones near Часов Яр Open (msg 660) .

ЦБСТ

  • 15.04.2025. ЦБСТ's channel, about the «Орбита» system: «Он уже интегрирован с тульским дроном «Овод» (нашим флагманским резидентом)» Primary doc (@ANO_CBST msg 623). The same post says the system let an operator in Moscow control a drone against real targets near Часов Яр.
  • 29.07.2026. After the shooting ЦБСТ writes of «Андрея Черезова, нашего боевого товарища и друга ЦБСТ», and that after the start of the war he «основал в Туле компанию по производству FPV-дронов, вошедшую впоследствии в контур ЦБСТ» Primary doc (msg 2366). This post says «контур» and «друга», not «резидент». It also states as fact that «Противник организовал покушение», with no evidence given Open.

What is not established

  • Who buys about ₽1.8bn of the 2024–2025 revenue that the four university contracts do not explain.
  • Why the balance sheet reached ₽4.22bn at the end of 2025.
  • The US designation of 2024: only The Insider reports it.
  • How РЛВТ and «Воздушный трактат» divide the work. The registry's earlier ЦБСТ-tie record sits on «Воздушный трактат»; ЦБСТ's post names only the drone, so which company is the resident is not stated.
  • Who «Андрей Иванов» is. On 26.03.2025 ЦБСТ's channel credits him as «создатель ударного FPV-дрона «Овод»»; the registry treats him as a separate documented engineer, and the relation between the accounts is not resolved.
  • Who shot Черезов and why.
  • Whether any of the combat claims on the company's channel are true. No purchase record connects the drones to a unit.
