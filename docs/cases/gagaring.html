Case study · ЦБСТ resident

Гагаринг: a company registered in September 2023 reports ₽2.16bn in 2025 revenue, is named a ЦБСТ resident by ЦБСТ itself, and appears in no public procurement record

The finding

ООО «Гагаринг» (ИНН 2312321660, Краснодар) was registered on 04.09.2023 [s6776]. Its filed accounts show revenue of ₽1.25m for 2023, ₽84.5m for 2024 and ₽2.16bn for 2025, with net profit of ₽664m in the last year [s0241, s0242, s0243]. Staff grew from 34 at the end of 2024 to 105 a year later [s5333]. Its comms system «Кузнечик» is reported as used at the front [s5436], and ЦБСТ's own channel calls the company a resident [s5440]. Two men own it: Александр Барашков (50.5%) and Алексей Александров (49.5%) [s0455]. Around it sit three part-owned drone and electronics companies, a co-founded training body that hands out diplomas, and an industry association whose director is Барашков (registry record ano-arrs-krasnodar). The related-party disclosures come from the audit report [s5333]. A search of zakupki.gov.ru for the company and seven of its network entities returns no hits.

Source: the three annual filings [s0241, s0242, s0243]; staff from the FY2025 audit report [s5333]. Revenue grew about 26 times in 2025, and net profit was 31% of revenue. The filing itself reports both the 2024 figure of ₽20.9m and the restated ₽15.4m; the reason for the restatement is not stated in the filings we read.

The audit report lists the related parties. Гагаринг owns 36.8% of ООО «Авирон», 50% of ООО «Интел Дрон» and 50% of ООО «НПО Эйлер», and is founder of АНО ДПО «Образовательный центр Гагаринг». Барашков holds 76% of ООО «СДЭК-НОВА» and 100% of ООО «СДЭК-КРАСНОДАР», and ООО «Дальсвязь» is affiliated with Александров [s5333].

The products

  • «Кузнечик» is a control and comms system for FPV drones, «от схемотехники до программного обеспечения» made by Гагаринг, according to a TASS wire item citing the Народный фронт, and reported as in use in the war zone (08.04.2025) Press-reported [s5436].
  • «Подхват» is a relay mode for «Кузнечик». ЦБСТ's channel: «Резидент ЦБСТ разработал режим управления БпЛА с «запредельной дистанцией» полёта» (02.05.2026) Primary doc [s5440]. The post says the mode «позволяет удвоить радиус боевой работы дронов и наземных роботизированных платформ» and requires two pilots, one launching and a second taking over near the line of contact.
  • Others on the registry record: ЮРКА detectors, «ГОРН» drone detector, «Звезда» jammers, «СОВА» video receiver, «СКАУТ» reconnaissance drones and the «Секира» FPV [s5437, s5444, s5445].
  • The army has not bought some of them, on Барашков's account. He says the «ГОРН» detector passed testing but troops are used to other detectors [s5439] Open.

Training, and the Архангел link

  • The training body. АНО ДПО «Образовательный центр Гагаринг» (ИНН 2310240630) was registered on 26.05.2025, founded by Барашков and by ООО «Гагаринг» [s6775, s5333].
  • Graduations. The Архангел channel reports a graduation of 03.08.2026: «Дипломы вручал директор КБ - Александр Сергеевич Барашков.» [s5372]. The registry also links Архангел Легион (ИНН 2315233718) to the training body, but that link carries no source id and is not re-verified here Open.
  • Joint project. «Архангел Спецназа», a channel of 1.02m subscribers, announced on 10.08.2026: «Совместно с лабораторией БПЛА «Гагаринг» команда Архангела Спецназа запускает проект для военнослужащих, завершивших службу, в том числе уволенных по ранению.» [s5392]
  • Cross-posting. Гагаринг's own channel @my_el_lab reposts Проект «Архангел» recruitment calls at least five times and carries a B2B pitch for Архангел interceptors on 30.08.2026 [s5539]. See the Архангел case.

State money and procurement

  • No procurement footprint. A zakupki.gov.ru search for Гагаринг and seven network entities on 05.09.2026 returned no hits for any of the eight ИНН (scan log). The company's own vacancy asks for «опыт работы в сфере ГОЗ не менее 2х лет» [s5419], which points to defence-order work that may not appear on the open portal. This is an inference, not a finding.
  • Not on the Минпромторг register. No current entry in the Минпромторг register of Russian radio-electronic products [s5421].
  • Sanctions. The registry holds no sanctions entry for the company or its owners.

What is not established

  • The customers behind ₽2.16bn of revenue. The filing names no counterparties for the 2025 revenue, and no public contract exists.
  • Why the 2024 profit was restated from ₽20.9m to ₽15.4m.
  • Whether the registered address is a residential block: the Rosreestr lookup is not archived.
  • Whether «Кузнечик» is used at the scale the TASS wire implies. The press item repeats a Народный фронт statement; no army purchase record is on file.
  • Whether the training body's centre is a 51st Army facility, as the registry record suggests, or a civilian school; that claim is not confirmed.
  • Whether «отряд Гагаринг» has any legal basis, and what «нам это одобрили» refers to.
  • The regional subsidy for equipment purchase that the registry lists is not verified from the sources read for this page.
