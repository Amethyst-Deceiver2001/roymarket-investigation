The finding

ООО «ГОУДРОН» (ИНН 9721203814) was registered in Moscow on 07.04.2023. One man, Козлов Дмитрий Сергеевич, owns 100% and is general director [s6768]. Its main ОКВЭД code is 46.43, wholesale of household electrical goods [s6768], and its registered address is an apartment, кв. 131, on ул. Ухтомского ополчения in Некрасовка [s6768]. It sells the «Набат» line of drone detectors under the GoDrone brand. The product site's own tagline is «Защита техники и личного состава в условиях СВО», and its price table has a separate soldiers' tier: ₽100,000 by bank transfer «военным», against ₽115,000 standard [s6457]. Its channel says «Мы работаем с ЦБСТ не первый месяц» [s7598]. Revenue was ₽86.3m in 2023, ₽178.3m in 2024 and ₽55.9m in 2025 [s0413, s0414, s0415]. No procurement record exists. Ukraine has listed the company under a presidential decree since 20.06.2025 [s7972].

The product

  • «Набат V.3». The product site gives a detection radius of up to 2 km, up to 7 drones tracked at once, and 6,800 mAh batteries lasting up to 9 hours [s6457]. Prices: retail ₽115,000; «Перевод (военным)» ₽100,000; «Карта / СБП (Военные)» ₽106,000; legal entities ₽120,000 with VAT [s6457].
  • Field claims are the company's own. Its channel posts soldier reviews: «Дрон-детектор «Набат V.2.C» спас жизни бойцов под Бахмутом.» (20.02.2025) and «НАБАТ.V3 доставлен на Запорожскую АЭС.» (26.12.2025) Open [s6458]. Its 29.07.2026 post reports that «Военнослужащими была дана высокая оценка работы устройства» after testing «в зоне проведения СВО и приграничных территориях» Open [s6458] (msg 3023). No unit is named in the testing post and no purchase record is on file.
  • One independent customer. A Yandex Maps review dated 20.02.2025 says the device was bought for servicemen and «свои задачи выполняет» Open [s1396].
  • Trademarks. Three Rospatent word marks, registered 12.02.2025, 20.02.2025 and 24.03.2026. Two cover detectors for unmanned aircraft and their repair and design [s1201, s1234]; the third covers a broad range of consumer electronics, drone accessories and retail services [s1207]. The product has a profile on this site.
