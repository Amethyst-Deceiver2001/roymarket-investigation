From ЦБСТ to the Front, in three stories

РОЙ.МАРКЕТ / ЦБСТ isn't one company — it's a network: a state-linked non-profit at the center, a cluster of manufacturers and distributors around it, and a handful of people who sit on several of those companies at once. Below are three of that network's best-documented chains, told as dated, individually-sourced claims — the same underlying evidence that backs this investigation's full technical record, just read in order instead of as a database.

How to read the tags: t-doc confirmed by a primary document t-press press-reported t-open open-source / social-media sourced, not yet document-confirmed

Story one

«Кузнечик» — a comms system, from workshop to front line

ООО «Гагаринг» (Краснодар, maker) → Russian units (field use, self-reported quantity 500 units)

Гагаринг makes the «Кузнечик» encrypted comms/control system for FPV drones, alongside 22 other UAV/EW products. Three separate, independently dated claims chart its path from an on-record press interview to a named front-line acknowledgment:

02.11.2024 t-press press self-report

Founder Александр Барашков, on record to RT: FPV drones fitted with «Кузнечик» have struck "hundreds" of military-equipment targets, including armored Western-made examples.

Source [s5435]

08.04.2025 t-press field confirmation

Independent republication via a state-linked movement and ТАСС confirms active battlefield use — while Барашков declines to give verifiable deployment-scale figures, calling them classified.

Source [s5436]

30.08.2026 t-open thank-you letter

A front-line-adjacent Telegram channel (@RuFront) posts a «благодарственное письмо» — the recognizable military thank-you-letter genre — thanking Гагаринг by name for 500 «Кузнечик» units.

Source [s5447]

Story two

«Булат» — one detector, sold twice, to two different kinds of buyer

ООО «Тримикс» → ООО «ПОКРОВ» → ИП Цыганов В.Б. (supply chain) → a federal МЧС spetsnaz unit, then a regional civilian administration

The «Булат» drone-detector line shows something the other chains don't: a full contract-to-delivery cycle, twice over, to two structurally different apex customers — a federal emergency-response spetsnaz unit, then an ordinary regional government.

29.10.2024 t-doc contract award

ИП Цыганов wins a sole-supplier contract with ГУ МЧС России по Курской области for 184 «Булат v.4» detector units, signed during the active Kursk-oblast incursion response.

₽16,192,000 · Source [s1416]

18.11.2024 t-doc delivery confirmation

A УПД transfer document confirms all 184 units actually delivered — closing the contract above with primary-document proof, not just a signed agreement.

Source [s1416]

18.09.2025 → 25.12.2025 t-doc contract → delivery

A second sole-supplier contract, this time with ФГКУ «ЦСООР «Лидер»» (МЧС spetsnaz), closes with an Акт signed by the unit's own commanding general — who names Цыганов explicitly as "Производитель" (manufacturer), not merely a reseller.

₽11,984,000 · Source [s1417, s1464]

2026 t-doc contract award

The same «Булат» product line sells again — this time to a regional civilian government, the Администрация Белгородского муниципального округа, not a military or emergency-response body.

₽495,000 · Source [s3424]

Story three

«Пиранья» — named by name, on two fronts

ООО «СКБ Пиранья» (Ulyanovsk, maker) → EU sanctions list · a named Russian brigade

Пиранья's «Пиранья 15/20» cargo-capable strike drones show a different kind of evidence: ЦБСТ's own channel naming Пиранья as one of six ЦБСТ-network residents added to the EU's 20th sanctions package, and a battlefield claim naming the actual receiving unit rather than an anonymous "front-line" recipient.

24.04.2026 t-doc sanctions designation, self-disclosed

ЦБСТ's own channel confirms Пиранья's inclusion in the EU's 20th sanctions package as an entity "входящее в контур Центра беспилотных систем и технологий" — a rare case where the network names its own sanctioned members.

ЦБСТ channel post 2159491523:2095

16.11.2024 t-open battlefield sighting

A ground-attack claim names a specific unit — «Бешеные псы» — and a specific target, a D-44 85mm gun, on the Pokrovsk axis, with a photo showing drones fitted with munitions.
