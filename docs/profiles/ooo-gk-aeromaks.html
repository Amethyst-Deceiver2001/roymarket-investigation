"SH-750" doesn't match any model currently in products[] on any of the three Аэромакс-named entities (ao-aeromaks: AS-32-10/AS-32-8/Д-12К fixed-wing/VTOL; ooo-aeromaks: B-120/B-200/B-700 helicopter-type, А-5/А-5 М/Л/A-50 aircraft/multicopter-type; ooo-gk-aeromaks: none listed). Given ooo-aeromaks already carries the B-series helicopter line and B-700 is noted as "in development — no dedicated page yet", SH-750 may be a renamed/rebranded designation for B-700 or a newer heavy model entirely — needs checking against the group's current product pages (aeromaks brand site/VK/Rosteh press) before wiring to any specific entity's products[].
