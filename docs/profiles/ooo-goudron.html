«Набат v3»

Drone detector, third iteration, tested 04.08.2026 — 200-8800 MHz detection range, also intercepts analog video signal. Per the entity's own dedicated product site (набат.рф, [s1393]): detection radius up to 2 km, tracks up to 7 drones simultaneously, 1.8" display, 2x removable 6800 mAh batteries, IP66, 5-9h battery life. Pricing tiers: retail 115,000₽; bank transfer (военным/"to military") 100,000₽; card/SBP standard 115,000₽; card/SBP military 106,000₽; legal entities 120,000₽ incl. VAT -- an explicit discounted military-buyer price tier, not just a generic bulk discount.
