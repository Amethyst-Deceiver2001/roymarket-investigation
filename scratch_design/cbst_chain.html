From ЦБСТ to the front

The earlier version of this exhibit drew every institutional tie at once — a 30-node star that showed that ЦБСТ sits at the center of this market but not how anything actually moves through it. This version drops that back to one diagram per case, and asks a narrower question of the data each time: what did ЦБСТ do, what did the resident make, and where is there real, sourced evidence it reached a battlefield or a government buyer? Read each row left to right. A chain that runs out — no named unit, no contract — stops there rather than being padded to look complete.

Every node and label below is a real registry record. Icons mark what kind of thing a node is (product category, from this project's own 17-category Product Icon Set; military unit or government customer, from its Demand-Side Icon Set) — nothing here is decorative. The full 29-entity institutional web this replaces is summarized, not deleted — see "the rest of the network" at the bottom.

The rest of the network

These five chains are not the whole institutional picture — 24 more entities carry a sourced cbst_tie record in the registry beyond these five, plus ЦБСТ's own 9-entity institutional core (funds, holding companies, a banking partner). That web is real and documented, but it answers a different question ("who is tied to ЦБСТ") than this page does ("what actually moved, and is there evidence it reached a battlefield or a government buyer").
