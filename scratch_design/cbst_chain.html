From ЦБСТ to the front

The earlier version of this exhibit drew every institutional tie at once — a 30-node star that showed that ЦБСТ sits at the center of this market but not how anything actually moves through it. This version drops that back to one diagram per case, and asks a narrower question of the data each time: what did ЦБСТ do, what did the resident make, and where is there real, sourced evidence it reached a battlefield or a government buyer? Read each row left to right. A chain that runs out — no named unit, no contract — stops there rather than being padded to look complete.

Every company, product and person node below is a real registry record; "battlefield use" and "government customer" nodes represent a sourced battlefield_evidence[] or government_contracts[] claim about that record, not a registry entity in their own right. Icons mark what kind of thing a node is (product category, from this project's own Product Icon Set; military unit or government customer, from its Demand-Side Icon Set) — nothing here is decorative. Every edge carries its own evidence grade — the same {doc}/{press}/{open} vocabulary the case studies use, pulled from each claim's actual source, not assumed. The full 29-entity institutional web this replaces is summarized, not deleted — see "the rest of the network" at the bottom.

The rest of the network

These five chains are not the whole institutional picture — 50 more entities carry a sourced cbst_tie record in the registry beyond the four of these five that have one, plus ЦБСТ's own 9-entity institutional core (funds, holding companies, a banking partner). That web is real and documented, but it answers a different question ("who is tied to ЦБСТ") than this page does ("what actually moved, and is there evidence it reached a battlefield or a government buyer").
