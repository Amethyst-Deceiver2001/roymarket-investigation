What changed since 15 September

This period was a sourcing and graph-integrity pass, not a discovery pass. Most of the movement is in how well existing claims are documented and in how the relationship graph is built, and it is listed in the order it matters for a sanctions submission.

  • Sanctions entries now cite a source. Of 18 sanctions[] entries that had no source_id (6 on entities, 12 on people), 15 now carry one — 5 entity entries and 10 person entries — each tied to an archived document and read by hand. Three are left unsourced on purpose: Лысогорский (the archived file's topics conflict with the note's own label), Скорятин (the record carries only a sanction.linked topic, not a designation) and ЦКБР (the claim rests on an Interfax item relayed by Чадаев). The registry now holds 63 sanctioned entities and 35 sanctioned people; the regime and date of designation were not re-confirmed against OFAC, EU, UK or GUR primary pages for any of them in this pass.
  • Dates of birth: 26 of 30 tied to a source. Each was matched by exact surname and date of birth against a row of the archived dossier (s1408) before the source was attached. Four remain open — Елисеев, Корчагина, Гаврилов, Пинимасова — and need a rupep or ЕГРЮЛ lookup that only you can run.
  • Roles: 30 of 101 sourcing gaps closed, 62 stubs separated out. The 62 records that looked like empty people were co-mention candidates from name extraction, not roles; they were moved to _scaffold_leads.json (66 entries) rather than deleted. Twelve roles on non-reference people still have no source.
  • ЦБСТ ties: 60 entities now carry a sourced tie (51 documentary, 7 open-source, 2 press), the 15 September exhibit counted 24 beyond the five case studies, and the same count is 50 now. Thirty-two ties gained a source id and nine new ties were written (ТСГ, НПП, ЭЛВО, Боевые технологии, VA Technologies, ЦУГАМ, Orient Systems, ЦКБР, ЭйАрТэк). Two claims are still unsourced: Воздушный трактат (via a product mention) and Килоуатт–Шестопалов (the post does not name the entity).
  • Graph policy: everything on the graph connects to the hub. The graph is now 1,095 nodes and 1,818 edges, down from 1,430 nodes. 40 minor people (22 relatives, 10 government signatories, 8 staff) are held on file with graph_tier: "reference" and resurface automatically if they gain an ownership, founder or director edge. A reachability check from the ЦБСТ hub drops every node it cannot reach — 287 ids at the last build — and lists them under detached_ids; the registry records themselves are untouched.
  • A founder-parsing bug hid real ownership. Multi-founder history strings joined with " / " lost every founder after the first. After the fix, 48 founder edges appeared and four garbage stub edges disappeared; among the recovered links were Аурин → КоптерЛаб and Проминвест → Атлант Аэро. The parser also now reads an ИНН written beside a founder name, which stops ООО «Аэромакс» and АО «Аэромакс» from being confused.
  • Noise stubs removed from the graph. The same free-text splitting had turned fragments of prose into fake "unresolved" people and companies — "000", "nominal ₽3", "— из 15 000 акций всего", "sole shareholder", "access-restricted" — 35 of the 159 stubs. Thousands separators are no longer split, role labels ("ГЕНЕРАЛЬНЫЙ ДИРЕКТОР: …") are stripped, multi-person director strings are split, and non-name fragments are refused. The numeric stubs had also been acting as false bridges: two records, ООО «Оупен Лоджистикс» and Диомидов В.В., had reached the hub only through them and are now correctly off the graph. 124 real unresolved names remain.
  • Detached sanctioned entities: five reconnected, seven still open. Левит, НОВА and Михельсон connect through the ДАР fund to Наша Правда (read from the FY2023 БФО note by OCR). Пехтра connects by press (special-prize winner in the accelerator, not a resident) and Взаимопомощь by open source (organiser of Дронница; an event tie only). НИИ СТТ connects through АО «Кронштадт» and ГК «Аэромакс». The seven with no sourced tie found: ГЭС-2, Элиарс, Интек Беспилотные Системы, Титан-Северо-Запад, Радиофид Системы, Архангел-Центр and Архангел-Мелитополь-2.
  • The Кронштадт group is on record properly, and an ownership inversion is fixed. АО «Кронштадт» (ИНН 7808035536) is a new entity, read from a fresh ЕГРЮЛ выписка. ООО «ГК Аэромакс» (formerly «Группа Кронштадт») was recorded as owned by ООО «Кронштадт Беспилотные Системы»; the primary выписка of the latter shows the opposite — ГК Аэромакс owns it, 100%. The current ownership of АО «Кронштадт» itself is not on any register we hold, and the group link rests on the group's own 18.05.2026 rename post. АО «Кронштадт» scores 6 of 27 on coverage and has not been screened against any list.
  • Presentation layer. The live Network Graph, Five Residents, From ЦБСТ to the Front and Multi-Role Individuals were refreshed against the 19 September graph. A profile index page lists all 205 company, 5 product and 366 person profiles, and dead links from profiles to pages that do not exist were removed.
01

The problem this audit was called to fix

Three times in one session, early in this audit's life, an entity got reported as an uncovered gap when it was already fully documented — each time from recollection rather than a registry read. That is not a memory problem to try harder at. An investigation of 200+ entities across a 7,500-file source corpus cannot be held in a head, and "have we checked X against Y?" has to be a query. Two artifacts make it one, and both remain the load-bearing tools behind every number here.

scripts/coverage_report.py

Scores every entity against 27 dimensions across five groups — identity, financial, screening, activity, analysis. --slug X gives a per-entity readout; --missing bfo emits a ready-to-work queue. Field-presence tests only, no judgement calls. Local, no network, safe to re-run — re-run fresh for this rewrite.

notes/SOURCE_REGISTER.md

The other half: every registry an entity should be checked against, each with an access tier probed and dated. "The user has to do this one" becomes a recorded property instead of something rediscovered each session. Not re-probed in this pass.

A missing dimension means not recorded, which is usually but not always not checked. Where the difference matters, a *_screened_at timestamp or a *_review field separates them: a null means never checked, a timestamp or closed-review record with an empty result is a genuine, citable negative. A field-presence score also says nothing about whether a claim is sourced — that is what section 00 tracks.

07

Proposed path to cutoff

The logic is unchanged; what moved is that the binding constraint is no longer only case-study evidence — it is now also the primary-source confirmation of sanctions.

  1. Stage 1 · unblocks everything else

    Clear the manual queue

    Items 1–5 in section 06. Item 1 changes what can be said on a slide; items 2, 3 and 5 are decisions only you can make and cost minutes.

  2. Stage 2 · nearly done

    Finish the scriptable sweeps

    БФО financials at 73%. Remaining: the entities without financials, screening for АО «Кронштадт», RNP across the full manufacturer roster, and the Fedresurs charter-capital pass on the case studies.

  3. Stage 3 · underway

    Deepen the five case studies only

    Still open: marketing-claims backfill for Пиранья, a government-contracts pass for Гагаринг and Пиранья, and the external document that separates Воздушный трактат from РЛВТ. Stay scoped to these five — the aggregate percentages in section 02 are not the target.

  4. Stage 4 · the deliverable

    Freeze and build the sanctions-gap list

    78 manufacturers currently carry no sanctions record; 47 of them have a documented revenue figure (52 have any financials row). Leaders by latest year: Смарт Текнолоджис (₽3.93bn, 2025), Гагаринг (₽2.16bn), Промсинтез (₽1.57bn), Специальные полезные технологии (₽1.44bn) and ПМЦ «Авангард» (₽1.28bn, 2022). That list, with growth curves attached, is the presentation's central exhibit. The 15 September figures were 77 and 53; the second is not comparable, since I could not reproduce that count's definition.

  5. Stage 5 · before anything goes public

    Adversarial read

    Every claim re-verified against its own source_id, aggregator-only sanctions matches upgraded to a primary designation read, and the PII decision applied. The material has to survive a hostile lawyer. Sourcing work in section 05 has moved most of this stage's inputs into place.

Suggested cutoff: unchanged — end of stage 3, but not before item 1 in section 06. Sanctions are the exhibit's spine, and their regime and date are the one thing still resting on aggregators. Everything after Stage 3 is assembly rather than investigation.
