Архангел: what the project says it's made of

Проект «Архангел» has published its own bill of materials, CAD screen captures and build-bench footage on its Telegram channel — this exhibit reproduces that footage directly and draws the airframe to the actual silhouette shown in it, not a generic drone shape.

What this is and isn't. The photographs below are the project's own published stills, reproduced as documentary evidence — the same standing as a press photo. The one drawn diagram reconstructs the silhouette actually visible in that footage (a slender tube body with a flared quad-leg skirt, "АРХАНГЕЛ" embossed on the tube — exactly as their own CAD screen shows it) so the shape is recognizable, not obscured. It states no dimension, wiring path, print parameter or assembly order. Registry record: proekt-kb-arkhangel.

Supply-chain watch — flagged for future entities

GNB / Gaoneng (Shenzhen Gaoneng New Energy, China) is the one component brand in this bill of materials that is not unique to Архангел — the same battery brand is independently on file in ooo-npo-rustekhdron's own litigation record, sourced completely separately from this one. Two unrelated actors in this registry, reaching for the same Chinese battery brand, is worth tracking as a pattern rather than treating each sighting as a one-off.

Every other named part in this bill of materials (Brotherhobby, HQProp, Caddx, GEPRC, SpeedyBee, BeastFPV, Bambu Lab) is so far unique to this one record — logged here so a future sighting of any of them elsewhere in the registry is recognized immediately rather than re-discovered.
