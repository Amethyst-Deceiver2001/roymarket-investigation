РОЙ.МАРКЕТ investigation · case-study exhibits

Five networks, one registry

Five fixed excerpts from the investigation's full relationship graph (data/registry/relationship_graph.json, 1,483 nodes / 1,984 edges) — one per flagship case study from the coverage audit's own prioritization. Each panel shows exactly that entity's real one-hop neighborhood: its people, its subsidiaries, its customers, and (summarized, see below) its products. Nothing here is summarized away or invented — every node, edge and figure is a registry record.

Hub circle size is not local graph density — a catalogue of 20 products used to make a hub look more important than a catalogue of 2, regardless of which company actually mattered more. Every hub here is instead sized by the same real number — latest known annual revenue, or the nearest verified figure where no revenue exists — on one shared scale across all five panels, so a bigger circle always means a bigger real figure, never a busier local diagram.

This is the fixed, read-only opening. The full interactive graph — search, filters, every entity's citation trail — is a separate tool for readers who want to dig past these five subjects on their own.

Products — icons, not spokes

Each panel shows up to 3 representative products from the registry's own Product Icon Set categories (scripts/classify_product_icon.py), plus a "+N more" count. A dashed square icon means the classifier found no matching category for that product. The full product name list is always printed beneath the icons — nothing is hidden, only de-emphasized visually.
