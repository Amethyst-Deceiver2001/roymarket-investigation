The ЦБСТ orbit

This investigation's thesis is that a single institution — АНО «ЦБСТ» — sits at the center of a marketplace of manufacturers, not that five manufacturers each happen to run their own independent network. This exhibit replaces an earlier "five independent hub-and-spoke diagrams" version of this content with what the underlying registry actually supports: one diagram, one center. ЦБСТ sits in the middle; its own institutional core (funds, its holding companies, a banking partner) forms an inner ring; every entity carrying a sourced, structured cbst_tie record — added to the graph schema for this exhibit, schema v4 — forms an outer ring of satellites. The five flagship case studies from the coverage audit are highlighted within that structure, not pulled out into their own diagrams.

Every node and every spoke shown is a real registry record — filtered and laid out for legibility, nothing invented. Where a flagship case study's own real connectivity does not run through a direct cbst_tie record, that is shown as it actually is (a real indirect path, or no traceable path at all) rather than forced into position — see cases 3b and 5 below.
