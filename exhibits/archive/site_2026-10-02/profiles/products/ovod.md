## Description

**«Овод»:** FPV strike-drone family, developed by Андрей Иванов (technical founder/spokesperson) under owner/director Черезов А.П. First batch of 4,000 units delivered to SVO forces Oct 2024. Per TheIns (29.07.2026, [s8058]), reporting on the assassination attempt against Черезов, verbatim: "Черезов владеет и управляет двумя компаниями... — ООО «Русская лаборатория воздушного транспорта» (РЛВТ) и ООО «Воздушный трактат» — обе они участвовали в разработке БПЛА «Овод»" (both companies participated in developing the «Овод» drone). [s8058]

**«Провод» / «Провод-15»:** Fiber-optic-guided heavy FPV strike drone, reported >40km range record in the SVO zone; press-reported destruction of a Ukrainian ammunition depot in Sumy region (Aug 2026 coverage). Brand appears more directly tied to sister entity РЛВТ (see ovod's own products[], sourced to [s8051]) -- TheIns's own article on this entity ([s8058]) covers «Овод» but does not separately name «Провод»/«Провод-15», so this entity's own participation in that specific variant remains unconfirmed beyond the general «Овод» family tie above. Left flagged, not force-sourced.
