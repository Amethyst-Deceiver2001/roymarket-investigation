The finding

ООО «ГОУДРОН» (ИНН 9721203814) was registered in Moscow on 07.04.2023. One man, Козлов Дмитрий Сергеевич, owns 100% and is general director . Its main ОКВЭД code is 46.43, wholesale of household electrical goods , and its registered address is an apartment, кв. 131, on ул. Ухтомского ополчения in Некрасовка . It sells the «Набат» line of drone detectors under the GoDrone brand. The product site's own tagline is «Защита техники и личного состава в условиях СВО», and its price table has a separate soldiers' tier: ₽100,000 by bank transfer «военным», against ₽115,000 standard . Its channel says «Мы работаем с ЦБСТ не первый месяц» . Revenue was ₽86.3m in 2023, ₽178.3m in 2024 and ₽55.9m in 2025 [, , ]. No procurement record exists. Ukraine has listed the company under a presidential decree since 20.06.2025 .

The product

  • «Набат V.3». The product site gives a detection radius of up to 2 km, up to 7 drones tracked at once, and 6,800 mAh batteries lasting up to 9 hours . Prices: retail ₽115,000; «Перевод (военным)» ₽100,000; «Карта / СБП (Военные)» ₽106,000; legal entities ₽120,000 with VAT .
  • Field claims are the company's own. Its channel posts soldier reviews: «Дрон-детектор «Набат V.2.C» спас жизни бойцов под Бахмутом.» (20.02.2025) and «НАБАТ.V3 доставлен на Запорожскую АЭС.» (26.12.2025) Open . Its 29.07.2026 post reports that «Военнослужащими была дана высокая оценка работы устройства» after testing «в зоне проведения СВО и приграничных территориях» Open (msg 3023). No unit is named in the testing post and no purchase record is on file.
  • One independent customer. A Yandex Maps review dated 20.02.2025 says the device was bought for servicemen and «свои задачи выполняет» Open .
  • Trademarks. Three Rospatent word marks, registered 12.02.2025, 20.02.2025 and 24.03.2026. Two cover detectors for unmanned aircraft and their repair and design [, ]; the third covers a broad range of consumer electronics, drone accessories and retail services . The product has a profile on this site.
